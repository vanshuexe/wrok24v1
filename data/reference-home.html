About Us

ABOUT SMART CUBES

At Smart Cubes, we believe creating a comfortable, functional space should be a simpler and smarter experience.

We specialise in purpose-built steel modular structures designed for modern living, leisure, and work. From compact studio spaces and 1 BHK or 2 BHK prefab homes to holiday cottages, farm cottages, office cabins, and site offices, our solutions are designed around practical needs and everyday usability.

Our approach brings manufacturing, transportation, and installation together in one streamlined process. With factory-controlled manufacturing, thoughtfully planned layouts, and modern interior options, we aim to make prefab construction a practical alternative for customers looking for a well-designed space without the complexity of a conventional build.

Whether you’re planning a weekend retreat, adding accommodation to a resort, or creating a functional workspace, Smart Cubes helps you take the next step with clarity and confidence.

Frequently asked questions

What are prefab homes?

Prefab homes are structures in which key components are manufactured or assembled in a factory and then transported to the installation site. Smart Cubes specialises in purpose-built steel modular structures designed for residential, hospitality, and commercial applications.

What types of prefab structures does Smart Cubes offer?

Smart Cubes offers ready-made designs for prefab homes, farm cottages, holiday cottages, studio spaces, office cabins, site offices, and security cabins. The most suitable option depends on your intended use and site requirements.

What sizes are available?

Our planned configurations include 12 ft × 20 ft and 12 ft × 40 ft units, along with 1 BHK and 2 BHK layouts. Available configurations and their specifications can be confirmed with our team.

How long does it take to get a prefab unit?

The indicative timeline is approximately 30–45 days, depending on the unit size and project requirements. The final schedule will be confirmed after discussing the specifications, site readiness, transportation, and installation requirements.

Can I use a prefab home on my farmland?

A prefab home or cottage may be an option for suitable farmland projects. However, permitted land use, construction approvals, access, utilities, and local regulations must be checked for your specific property before installation.

Are prefab homes suitable for Bangalore's weather?

Steel modular structures can be designed with suitable insulation, ventilation, windows, and waterproofing measures. Actual indoor comfort depends on the complete design, materials, orientation, and installation quality. Discuss your site's conditions with our team to understand the available specifications.

Can I customise the design?

Smart Cubes primarily offers ready-made designs. Available sizes, layouts, finishes, and features can be discussed with our team to determine which options suit your requirements.

What features are included in a prefab home?

Depending on the selected model and agreed specifications, available features may include a bathroom, plumbing, kitchen, electrical fittings, air conditioning, insulation, furniture, and large windows. Please confirm the inclusions and exclusions in your quotation.

How much does a Smart Cubes prefab home cost?

Pricing depends on the size, layout, interior features, specifications, transportation distance, and installation requirements. Contact Smart Cubes for a quotation based on your intended use and project location.

Does Smart Cubes handle transportation and installation?

Yes. Smart Cubes' service offering covers design selection, manufacturing, transportation, and installation. The exact scope, site preparation requirements, and related charges should be confirmed in the project quotation.

Does the quoted price include transportation, foundation, and installation?

These inclusions depend on the final project quotation. Before confirming an order, ask for an itemised breakdown covering the unit, transportation, installation, foundation or site preparation, utilities, and any additional charges.

What maintenance does a steel modular structure require?

Maintenance depends on the materials, protective coatings, exposure to weather, waterproofing, plumbing, and electrical installations. Smart Cubes can provide product-specific maintenance guidance once the final specifications and after-sales support arrangements are confirmed.

Do prefab homes come with a warranty?

Warranty terms and after-sales support are currently to be confirmed. Please contact our team for the applicable terms before placing an order.

Do I need permission to install a prefab home?

Permissions depend on the property's location, land classification, intended use, applicable building regulations, and local authority requirements. A prefab structure should not be assumed exempt from approvals simply because it is factory-manufactured. Check the requirements for your site before proceeding.

How do I get started with Smart Cubes?

Getting started is simple. Share your intended use, preferred size, installation location, and approximate budget through our enquiry form. Our team can then discuss the available designs, specifications, estimated cost, and next steps.
