# Design gallery imagery

The gallery uses a different architectural concept image for each of its five categories. These illustrations do not represent completed Smart Cubes projects.

Two images were generated with the built-in image generation tool to replace repeated gallery photos:

- Studio and compact spaces: `public/images/studio-v2.jpg`
- Site offices and security cabins: `public/images/site-office-v2.jpg`

The same category image is used consistently on Home, Designs, and the corresponding design dialog.

## Studio prompt

Create a photorealistic architectural concept photo for a modular prefab website, landscape 1536x1024. A genuinely COMPACT single-room studio home, only about 12 x 20 feet, off-white ribbed metal walls, slim dark bronze framing, one large corner window and a separate simple timber door, flat roof with a thin overhang. Visible cozy bed and a tiny kitchenette through the window. Set in a small peaceful urban courtyard garden with gravel, potted plants, a narrow concrete patio and tall bamboo privacy fence. Bright soft morning light, clean contemporary architectural editorial photography, three-quarter view showing the whole small building. Make it visibly a compact studio, distinctly different from a large timber cottage, resort cabin or glass office. Warm realistic materials, natural neutral colors. No people, text, watermarks or logos. Illustrative concept image.

## Site office prompt

Create a photorealistic architectural concept photo for a modular buildings website, landscape 1536x1024. A practical purpose-built SITE OFFICE AND SECURITY CABIN at the entrance of an organised Indian construction site. Main portable steel office is a long rectangular unit in light grey corrugated steel with teal corner trims and teal door, three ordinary sliding rectangular windows with white frames, a modest metal entrance stair, a single outdoor air-conditioning unit. A small separate matching compact security kiosk and simple boom barrier visible beside it. Background: neat gravel access road, partially constructed concrete building far behind, safety fence and subtle construction crane. Clean professional realistic construction photography in daylight, front three-quarter view. Clearly utilitarian portable steel modules, not a luxury glass garden cabin, not a wooden house. No readable text, logos, watermarks or people. Illustrative concept.
